About 01

I design and build the pipelines that turn scattered, messy sources into data a business can actually use. My work covers ingestion from relational databases, files and APIs, transformation through Bronze–Silver–Gold layers on Azure, and the modelling that makes the result queryable. A pipeline is only finished when it holds up under real data.

I came to data engineering through machine learning, and that shapes how I work: I have deployed models on embedded hardware, automated training and release with CI/CD pipelines, and served LLM-based systems as containerised APIs. I know what downstream consumers need from a dataset because I have been one.

Alongside this, I am completing a PhD on AI applied to medical imaging at the SETIME laboratory, and I teach the Deep Learning lab sessions of the "AI and Connected Objects" Master's programme at Ibn Tofail University.
